# Handoff

- **Outcome and owner** — Assign the remaining delivery and its observable finish line to the receiving session.
- **Context** — Link the issue, current worktree or revision, evidence, and any dependency or resumption trigger.
- **Authority** — Carry the existing grants, limits, and user gates needed to finish the work.

For managed launches, provide the verified initiative, member, request, lead, and destination identifiers required by the [selected integration](../skills/manage/references/sessions.md#launch-interface).
