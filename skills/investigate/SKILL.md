# investigate

Follow the [working agreement](../../references/working-agreement.md).

- Find evidence that distinguishes causes, measures impact, and supports a mitigation.
- Check scope and side effects before running diagnostics, and trial uncertain experiments in isolation before scaling.
- Use [monitor](../monitor/SKILL.md) for bounded observation, [Datadog guidance](references/datadog.md) for Datadog, and [execute](../execute/SKILL.md) for assigned recovery.
