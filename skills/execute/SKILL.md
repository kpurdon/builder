# execute

Follow the [working agreement](../../references/working-agreement.md).

Determine and record the finish line and required evidence from the request, governing record, and repository policy. Completion may mean a report, reviewed draft PR, merged change, or verified rollout. Use the [PR default](references/pr-delivery.md) only when those sources leave PR delivery unspecified. Resolve consequential ambiguity; handle routine steps independently. Required gates do not grant merge or deployment authority.

Own the work and waits through completion, including applicable review, fixes, CI, authorized rollout, and [validation](../validate/SKILL.md). A PR is an intermediate result when the assignment extends further. Use [design](../design/SKILL.md) for consequential choices and independent [review](../review/SKILL.md) for non-PR work when required or warranted by its consequences.

For PR descriptions and reviewer handoffs, use the [description guidance](references/pr-description.md). A description-only request ends with draft text unless publication is requested.

For handoff briefs, use [session operations](../manage/references/sessions.md). For delegated work, follow its [completion reporting contract](../manage/references/sessions.md#completion-reporting).

Use relevant [operations](references/operations.md) and [Datadog guidance](../investigate/references/datadog.md); [investigate](../investigate/SKILL.md) unexpected results.
