# review

Follow the [working agreement](../../references/working-agreement.md).

- Review the target revision against the requested outcome, repository requirements, and consequential constraints.
- Reuse independent review evidence when its scope and revision fit, and request focused follow-up for material gaps.
- Report actionable findings with impact and evidence, leaving edits and publication to the authorized owner.
