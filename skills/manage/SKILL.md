# manage

Follow the [working agreement](../../references/working-agreement.md).

Organize the outcome into independently owned deliveries in the [project record](../../templates/project.md), following [record maintenance](references/sessions.md#current-state-records). Tickets and files do not imply separate owners. Use [design](../design/SKILL.md) for unresolved design or planning work. Give each executor the outcome, context, constraints, authority, and usable dependencies; it determines and owns [delivery completion](../execute/SKILL.md).

Dispatch work once prerequisites, decisions, and authority are ready; keep blocked work queued. Use the [session operations guidance](references/sessions.md) to prepare and launch dedicated execution sessions. Keep implementation out of the lead session and reserve its subagents for bounded audits, investigations, or reviews. Surface launch blockers without taking over execution.

Follow the [completion reporting contract](references/sessions.md#completion-reporting). Work on other ready deliveries or wait for completion. Use completion evidence to update the record and unblock dependent work. Retain shared scope and dependency decisions, assign uncovered work, and close the project when completed deliveries satisfy its acceptance criteria.
