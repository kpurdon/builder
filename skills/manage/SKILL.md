# manage

Follow the [working agreement](../../references/working-agreement.md).

Organize the outcome into independently owned deliveries in the [project record](../../templates/project.md), following [record maintenance](references/sessions.md#current-state-records). Tickets and files do not imply separate owners. Give each executor the outcome, context, constraints, authority, and usable dependencies; it determines and owns [delivery completion](../execute/SKILL.md). Design, investigation, implementation, review, and validation are stages an owner may perform, not reasons for a new assignment. Use [design](../design/SKILL.md) for shared scope and dependency decisions.

Dispatch work once prerequisites, decisions, and authority are ready; keep blocked work queued. Use the [session operations guidance](references/sessions.md) to prepare and launch dedicated execution sessions. Keep implementation out of the lead session and reserve its subagents for bounded audits, investigations, or reviews. Surface launch blockers without taking over execution.

Follow the [completion reporting contract](references/sessions.md#completion-reporting). Work on other ready deliveries or wait for completion. Assess completion evidence against the delivery's acceptance criteria; return unresolved findings to its owner before accepting completion. Update the record, unblock dependent work, and perform authorized [session retirement](references/sessions.md#retirement). Coordinate shared decisions and summarize project status while executors retain delivery obligations. Assign uncovered work and close the project when completed deliveries satisfy its acceptance criteria.
